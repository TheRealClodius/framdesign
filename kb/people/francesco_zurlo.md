## Overview

Francesco Zurlo is a prominent figure in Italian design theory and the Dean of the School of Design at Politecnico di Milano. He is an expert in strategic design, design management, and the intersection of design with organizational systems.

## Collaboration with Andrei Clodius

Francesco Zurlo was Andrei Clodius's professor at the Politecnico di Milano. They collaborated on the book *Strategie del Design*, where Andrei worked closely with Zurlo to poetically interpret complex strategic concepts into the book's signature illustrations and visual metaphors.

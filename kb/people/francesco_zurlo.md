## Bio

Francesco Zurlo is the Dean of the School of Design at Politecnico di Milano. His research focuses on strategic, systematic, and creative research through design, with attention to the ecological impact of business innovations and human flourishing. He is the author of numerous international publications on strategic design, design-driven innovation, and design for organisations.

## Background

Zurlo is the founder and director of CI.Lab, a research unit within the Design Department at Politecnico di Milano focused on the dynamics and competitiveness of the creative industries. He is a member of the scientific committee of the Observatory of Design Thinking for Business at Politecnico's School of Management, and of ADI Index, the most important organisation for assessing the best design in Italy.

He directs the Executive Master in Design Strategy and System Innovation and co-directs the Master in Strategic Design and Furniture Design at Politecnico di Milano.

## Collaboration with Andrei Clodius

Francesco Zurlo was Andrei's professor and coordinator at Politecnico di Milano. He approached Andrei to collaborate on the book *Strategie del Design*, where Andrei contributed to researching the content and concepts and created the book's illustrations — visual metaphors interpreting complex strategic and organisational design frameworks.

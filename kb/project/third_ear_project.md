## Overview

Third Ear is an industrial design concept for a wearable audio device carried around the neck, combining recording and listening in a single product. The system pairs a neck-worn casing with earbuds that act as microphones and playback devices, enabling immersive audio capture.

## Context and problem

Portable audio recording is often treated as a separate workflow from listening. Third Ear explores a more natural form factor that allows users to capture and replay sound on the go, with an emphasis on spatial audio and discreet hardware presence.

## Role and scope

Andrei led the concept and industrial design exploration, focusing on:

- **Earbud form factor**: Shape explorations for comfortable, compact earbuds.
- **Casing design**: Necklace-mounted casing concepts, textures, and patterns.
- **Connector system**: A dedicated connector between casing and earbuds to support high-quality audio input.
- **Status signaling**: Subtle LED indicators to communicate device state without implying active recording.

## What was built

- Concept sketches for earbuds, casing shapes, and surface textures.
- Connector ideas supporting multi-microphone input for spatial capture.
- Visual renders exploring material and lighting cues.

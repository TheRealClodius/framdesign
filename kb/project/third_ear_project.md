## Overview

Third Ear is a wearable audio device carried around the neck, combining recording and listening in a single product. The system pairs a neck-worn casing with earbuds that act as both microphones and playback devices, enabling immersive spatial audio capture. The project was the reason Andrei went to HAX (HAXLR8R) in Shenzhen, China, where it received $60,000 USD in funding to build a prototype for a crowdfunding campaign.

## Context and problem

Portable audio recording is treated as a separate workflow from listening, typically requiring dedicated equipment. Third Ear explored a more natural form factor that allows users to capture and replay sound on the go, with emphasis on spatial audio and a discreet hardware presence that does not signal "recording" to people nearby.

## Role and scope

Andrei led the entire concept and industrial design, working within the HAX hardware accelerator ecosystem in Shenzhen:

- **Earbud form factor**: Shape explorations for comfortable, compact earbuds functioning as both microphones and playback devices.
- **Casing design**: Necklace-mounted casing concepts, textures, and surface patterns.
- **Connector system**: A dedicated connector between casing and earbuds to support high-quality multi-microphone input for spatial audio capture.
- **Status signaling**: Subtle LED indicators to communicate device state without implying active recording.
- **Factory visits and supplier meetings**: Navigating the Shenzhen manufacturing landscape — visiting factories, meeting suppliers, and building prototypes under real production constraints.

## What was built

- **Prototype**: A working prototype developed during the HAX program with $60,000 USD in funding.
- **Earbud and casing designs**: Concept sketches and visual renders for earbuds, casing shapes, surface textures, and material explorations.
- **Spatial audio connector**: Connector concepts supporting multi-microphone input for spatial capture.
- **Manufacturing research**: Direct engagement with the Shenzhen supply chain — factories, component suppliers, and production feasibility studies.

## Outcomes

The prototype was built during the HAX program. The planned crowdfunding campaign did not happen and the project did not progress to market.

## What this project represents

Third Ear is the project that brought Andrei to Shenzhen and into the hardware accelerator world. Beyond the product itself, it was a formative experience in navigating manufacturing at scale — factory visits, supplier negotiations, and prototyping under real constraints. It was also a significant personal growth experience. The project represents early wearable thinking and the beginning of Andrei's hands-on relationship with production processes, a thread that continued through Vector Watch, Fitbit, and into FRAM's current approach of working directly with the material.

## Overview

CELIA is a line of thermostats and heaters designed for Leroy Merlin. The project includes premium aluminum variants, add-on thermostat modules, and designs tailored for integration in home interiors.

## Role and scope

Andrei led the industrial design for the CELIA line, defining:

- **Thermostat module design**: Interface and physical detailing for core controls.
- **Premium aluminum header**: Material exploration and detailing for premium models.
- **Add-on integration**: Modular thermostat components for different heater models.
- **Home context**: Visual integration of heaters within residential environments.

## What was built

- Multiple heater and thermostat variants within the CELIA line.
- Premium aluminum heater designs with integrated smart thermostat modules.
- Add-on thermostat modules compatible with CELIA heaters.

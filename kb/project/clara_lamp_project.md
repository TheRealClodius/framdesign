## Overview

Clara Lamp is an industrial design project by Andrei exploring a sculptural lighting object. The project includes assembly documentation and long-exposure photography to capture the lamp's presence and light behavior.

## Role and scope

Andrei led the design and presentation of the lamp, collaborating with artist Mihai Topescu during assembly and documenting the lamp through staged photography.

## What was built

- A finished lamp design with multiple photo studies.
- Assembly documentation and portrait-style imagery.

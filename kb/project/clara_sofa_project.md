## Overview

Clara Sofa is a lounge sofa with an integrated overhead light, designed as an entry for a Flos competition. The light references the half-sphere shape of the Flos Arco lamp by Achille Castiglioni. The overhead light uses WRGB LEDs and can shift to any color based on time of day or the surrounding environment's color palette. Materials include wood, metal, textile, and LED. The project is part of the Clara series alongside the Clara Lamp.

## Context and problem

Designed post-university as a competition entry for Flos. The brief called for a furniture-lighting hybrid, and Andrei explored how ambient, color-responsive lighting could be integrated directly into a lounge form factor rather than treated as a separate fixture.

## Role and scope

Andrei led the full concept and form exploration, developing the sofa as a standalone industrial design study — from material selection and structural design to the responsive lighting system.

## What was built

- **Lounge sofa with integrated overhead light**: A single piece combining seating and ambient illumination.
- **Castiglioni-inspired light element**: Half-sphere overhead light referencing the Flos Arco lamp's formal language.
- **WRGB LED system**: Color-adaptive lighting capable of shifting based on time of day or surrounding environment color palette.
- **Multi-material construction**: Wood, metal, textile, and LED components composed into a unified form.

## Outcomes

The project remained as a concept and did not place in the Flos competition.

## What this project represents

Clara Sofa is a formative exercise from Andrei's early independent practice, exploring the integration of responsive lighting into furniture form. It is part of the Clara series alongside the Clara Lamp.

## Overview

Playable was a venture studio founded by Andrei that began during the crypto wave and evolved into a space for building and launching his own products. It started by working with early-stage crypto startups on visual and product direction, then transitioned into an independent product studio. Playable eventually became FRAM Design.

## Context and problem

The crypto boom of 2022 created demand for rapid visual and product direction among early-stage startups. Andrei created Playable to meet that demand, working with multiple crypto ventures on branding and design direction. As the crypto market cooled and Andrei's interests shifted toward building his own products, Playable evolved from a service studio into a product incubator.

## Role and scope

Andrei led the entire initiative as Studio Director, managing a team of five domain experts spanning brand design, UX, and front-end development:

- **Crypto phase**: Guiding visual direction and product design for early-stage crypto startups. Work was primarily branding and visual design rather than deep product development.
- **Product phase**: Transitioning Playable into a vehicle for building and launching Andrei's own products, including Job Compass and Semantic Space.
- **Studio evolution**: Shaping the studio's identity and focus as it moved from client services to independent product work, eventually becoming FRAM Design.

## What was built

- **Crypto startup collaborations**: Visual direction and product design for multiple early-stage crypto ventures, most notably Virtual Pangea.
- **Virtual Pangea**: Design and product direction work that contributed to the project securing a $3 million USD seed round.
- **Job Compass**: An AI-driven recruitment tool using agent-based matching (now paused, looking for funding).
- **Semantic Space**: An ongoing project that carried forward into FRAM.
- **Studio infrastructure**: A team of five domain experts (brand, UX, front-end) and processes that later formed the foundation of FRAM Design.

## Outcomes

Playable's most notable outcome was the Virtual Pangea work, which contributed to a $3 million USD seed round. The studio successfully transitioned from crypto-era client work into independent product development. The team, processes, and product vision evolved directly into FRAM Design, with the team structure becoming flexible — Andrei leads the work and scales the team based on the type of collaboration.

## What this project represents

Playable represents the bridge between Andrei's service-oriented studio work and FRAM's current model as a product lab. It demonstrates the ability to pivot a studio's focus from trend-driven client work to self-directed product development, carrying forward the team and capabilities while sharpening the mission. FRAM is the direct successor to Playable.

## Overview

Organic Space Separators is a collection of sculptural room dividers designed for Novotel hotel lounges in France. The separators feature freeform organic shapes informed by an ecologically focused temporary branding direction that Novotel was exploring at the time. The panels were produced in a limited series.

## Context and problem

Novotel was pursuing a temporary branding refresh with an ecological focus for its hotel lounges in France. The brief called for space-defining elements that could divide open reception and lounge areas while reinforcing the brand's visual direction.

## Role and scope

Andrei led the design of the collection, defining the organic forms, material choices, and production approach for the hospitality environment.

## What was built

- **Sculptural room dividers**: Freeform organic panels designed to define space in open hotel lounges.
- **Sandblasted glass panels**: Translucent glass elements with sandblasted finishing for diffused light and visual privacy.
- **Robotically painted metal structure**: Metal framework with robotic paint application for consistent finish quality at production scale.
- **Limited production series**: Panels were manufactured and installed in Novotel hotel lounges in France.

## Outcomes

The design was selected from the pitch and moved into production. Panels were manufactured in a limited series and installed in Novotel hotel lounges in France.

## What this project represents

Organic Space Separators is an early example of Andrei's work at the intersection of industrial design and interior environments, translating brand direction into physical, functional objects produced at scale.

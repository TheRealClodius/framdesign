## Overview

UrbanAir (originally pitched as Neobility) is a mobility aggregator designed to simplify urban transportation. The core vision is based on the premise that the future of mobility is multi-modal, requiring a flexible network of alternative options like ridesharing, vehicle renting, and e-scooters to supplement traditional public transport.

## Context and Problem

Urban environments are increasingly crowded with fragmented mobility services. Users often have to juggle multiple apps to find, book, and unlock different types of vehicles (bikes, scooters, cars). This fragmentation creates friction and prevents the emergence of a truly seamless, multi-modal transportation network.

## The Solution

UrbanAir acts as a "one app to unlock all" platform. It aggregates all available e-scooters, bikes, and cars for rent in a specific city into a single interface. By providing a unified view and access point, it enables users to choose the best mode of transport for their specific journey without needing to manage multiple accounts or applications.

## Key Concepts

- **Multi-modal Integration**: Combining various forms of private and shared transport into a coherent network.
- **Aggregation Strategy**: Putting together all available local mobility options.
- **Unified Access**: A single digital key and interface for multiple hardware providers.
- **Flexible Network**: Designing for a transportation ecosystem that is both powerful and adaptable to user needs.

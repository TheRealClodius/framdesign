## Overview

UrbanAir is a mobility aggregator that unifies all available e-scooters, bikes, and cars for rent in a city into a single app. Built by Neobility, where Andrei was the first employee and Product Director, UrbanAir's core feature — Universal Unlock — lets users access vehicles from multiple hardware providers with a single digital key. The app is live and available on app stores.

## Context and problem

Urban environments are increasingly crowded with fragmented mobility services. Users juggle multiple apps to find, book, and unlock different types of vehicles. This fragmentation prevents the emergence of seamless multi-modal transportation. Neobility was founded to solve this by aggregating all local mobility options into one interface.

## Role and scope

Andrei was the first employee at Neobility and helped secure the initial seed round of $650,000 USD. He acted as Product Director, informing every product decision for UrbanAir:

- **Product direction**: Defined the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for the aggregation platform.
- **Initial UX design**: Designed the foundational user experience before hiring a larger team.
- **Universal Unlock**: Conceived and directed the core feature — a single digital key and interface for unlocking vehicles from multiple hardware providers.
- **Team building**: Helped grow the team to 15 people at peak, primarily in software development.
- **Five app releases**: Directed five major updates over the Neobility period.

## What was built

- **Mobility aggregator app**: A single interface aggregating all available e-scooters, bikes, and cars for rent in a given city.
- **Universal Unlock**: One digital key that works across multiple vehicle providers — the defining feature of the product.
- **Multi-modal integration**: A system combining various forms of private and shared transport into a coherent network.
- **Unified access layer**: Single account and payment system across all integrated mobility providers.

## Outcomes

UrbanAir shipped and reached 10,000 daily active users. The team grew to 15 people. The app is still live and available on app stores. Andrei directed five major releases during his time as Product Director.

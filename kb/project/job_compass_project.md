## Overview

Job Compass is an intelligent recruitment tool that uses teams of AI agents to find unorthodox matches between candidates and businesses. Rather than relying on automated keyword filters that exclude otherwise strong candidates, Job Compass analyses both business context and candidate context qualitatively to surface fits that traditional systems miss. The name refers to showing "true North" on a candidate or a business — revealing potential that conventional filtering overlooks.

## Context and problem

The recruitment space relies heavily on automated filters that screen candidates by keyword matching against job descriptions. This approach routinely excludes people who would be a strong fit if their experience and skills were evaluated through a different lens. Qualified candidates fall through the cracks, and businesses miss out on talent that does not conform to rigid filtering criteria.

## Role and scope

Job Compass originated at Playable, the venture studio Andrei led. He drove the full product vision, interface design, and proof-of-concept development:

- **Product vision**: Defining the agent-based matching concept — teams of agents analysing both sides of the recruitment equation.
- **Interface design**: Onboarding motion studies, UI concepts, and the metaphorical "compass" interaction model.
- **PoC development**: Launching a working proof-of-concept to validate the approach in real recruitment scenarios.

## What was built

- **Agent-based matching system**: A concept where multiple agents analyse business context (culture, team dynamics, actual needs) and candidate context (experience, skills, trajectory) independently, then find non-obvious connections.
- **Compass interface**: A UI concept centered on the metaphor of finding "true North" — guiding users toward matches they would not have found through conventional search.
- **Onboarding flow**: Motion-driven onboarding experience introducing users to the qualitative matching approach.

## Outcomes

The proof-of-concept was built and launched during the Playable period (2022–2023). The project is currently paused and looking for funding. The timing was not right at launch, but the rise of AI-assisted workflows has since shifted skill sets significantly — a qualitative approach to match-making may be exactly what certain businesses and candidates need today. The project may be revisited with the right partnership.

## What this project represents

Job Compass represents FRAM's ability to identify structural problems in established industries and propose agent-driven alternatives. It demonstrates early thinking around agentic automation applied to a human-centered problem — matching people to opportunities through qualitative understanding rather than keyword reduction.

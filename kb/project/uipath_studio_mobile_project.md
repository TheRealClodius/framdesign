## Overview

UiPath Studio Mobile is a design exploration into transposing the powerful automation building capabilities of the UiPath ecosystem to mobile devices. The goal is to allow automation developers and business users to test, edit, and manage their workflows on the go, providing flexibility outside the traditional desktop environment.

## Context and Problem

Automation building has traditionally been a desktop-bound activity due to the complexity of workflow visualization and the precision required for configuration. However, as automation becomes more integrated into daily business operations, there is an increasing need for users to be able to make quick adjustments or verify workflow execution without needing to be at their desks.

## Design Exploration

The exploration focused on how to condense complex automation logic into a mobile-friendly format:

- **Step-by-Step Editing**: Designing an interface that allows users to focus on and edit a single specific step within an automation workflow, ensuring clarity on a smaller screen.
- **Visual Consistency**: Ensuring that the mobile experience feels like a natural extension of the desktop Studio while optimizing for touch interactions.
- **Adaptive Themes**: Implementing dark and light mode explorations to ensure usability across different lighting conditions and user preferences.
- **Mobile Workflow Testing**: Allowing users to trigger and monitor automation runs directly from their phones, facilitating rapid iteration and troubleshooting.

## Overview

UiPath Studio Mobile is a design exploration into transposing UiPath's automation building capabilities to mobile devices. The work was part of broader internal discussions on how to evolve UiPath Studio to be more user-friendly, exploring whether complex automation workflows could be condensed into a mobile-first format.

## Context and problem

Automation building has traditionally been a desktop-bound activity due to the complexity of workflow visualisation and the precision required for configuration. As automation becomes more integrated into daily business operations, there is an increasing need for users to make quick adjustments or verify workflow execution without being at their desks. The exploration asked: what would UiPath Studio look like if it had to work on a phone?

## Role and scope

Andrei led the design exploration, focusing on how to simplify UiPath Studio's complexity for a mobile context:

- **Step-by-step editing**: An interface allowing users to focus on and edit a single step within an automation workflow, optimised for smaller screens.
- **Visual consistency**: Ensuring the mobile experience felt like a natural extension of desktop Studio while adapting for touch interactions.
- **Adaptive themes**: Dark and light mode explorations for usability across conditions.
- **Mobile workflow testing**: Allowing users to trigger and monitor automation runs directly from their phones.

## Outcomes

The exploration contributed to internal discussions on the evolution of UiPath Studio's user experience. It remained as a design study and was not developed further.

## What this project represents

Studio Mobile is an example of Andrei applying mobile product thinking to a traditionally desktop-bound enterprise tool. It reflects the approach of questioning platform assumptions — asking whether a complex professional tool can be fundamentally simplified rather than just ported to a new form factor.
